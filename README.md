# 💊 PILLUMINATI
### Because remembering meds shouldn't be a family conspiracy.

PILLUMINATI is an accessible, caregiver-friendly medication management platform designed to help elderly individuals stay on track with their medications while keeping their loved ones informed and reassured.

Built for **PromptWars × Catalyst Hackathon**.

---

## 🌟 Overview

Medication adherence can be challenging for elderly individuals, especially when managing multiple prescriptions, complicated schedules, or living independently.

PILLUMINATI bridges this gap through a simple, supportive digital experience that combines medication reminders, caregiver coordination, AI-powered assistance, and emergency health information.

## ✨ Features

- **Medication Management** – Create and manage medication schedules, dosages, frequencies, and instructions.
- **Dose Tracking** – Mark medications as taken and maintain adherence history.
- **Care Relay** – Coordinate caregivers and notify family members about missed or unconfirmed doses.
- **Prescription OCR** – AI-assisted prescription scanning to extract medication details for human verification.
- **Emergency Health Passport** – QR-based access to essential emergency health information.
- **AI Health Companion** – Friendly health check-ins and medication-related assistance.
- **Grandma's Voice** – Voice-enabled interactions for improved accessibility.
- **Pillu 🐾** – A friendly companion that makes medication management more engaging and encouraging.
- **Appointment Management** – Keep track of upcoming medical appointments.

## 🎯 Our Mission

To make medication management simpler, safer, and more accessible for elderly individuals while reducing the stress experienced by caregivers and families.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- Frontend: React / TypeScript
- Styling: Tailwind CSS
- Backend & Authentication: Firebase
- Database: Cloud Firestore
- AI: Google Gemini
- Deployment: Vercel

## 👥 Target Users

- Elderly individuals managing daily medications
- Family members monitoring medication adherence
- Caregivers coordinating medication schedules
- Individuals requiring accessible medication management

## 🔐 Safety & Privacy

- Prescription extraction is intended as a draft requiring human verification.
- PILLUMINATI does not replace medical professionals or prescribe medication.
- Emergency information is intended to assist responders and caregivers.
- Sensitive health information should only be accessible to authorized users.

## 💡 Vision

We envision a future where elderly individuals can maintain independence without compromising their safety, and families can care without constantly worrying.
